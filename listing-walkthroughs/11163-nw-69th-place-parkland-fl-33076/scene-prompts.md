# In-App Generation Guide — 9 Cinematic Scenes

Generate each scene in the Higgsfield app with **image-to-video** (DoP), **5 seconds**,
using the matching scene image and prompt below. Then attach the 9 finished MP4s in chat
— they'll be stitched into the 16:9 master and 9:16 social cut automatically.

Every prompt already includes the style rules from the reference reel (gimbal, head-height,
photorealistic, nothing invented).

**Shared style tail (already appended to each prompt below):**
"Smooth, slow, continuous single take at realistic head height, like a professional real
estate videographer operating a stabilized gimbal. Preserve the exact architecture, layout,
furniture, finishes, colors and lighting of the source photo. Natural daylight,
photorealistic, realistic depth and parallax. Do not add people, objects, text or logos.
No cuts, no floating camera, no distortion, no camera shake."
